    Protein

    Mass is the average mass of the chain. pI uses the Bjellqvist pK values, the same as ExPASy ProtParam. A280 of a 1 mg/mL solution assumes all cysteines reduced (5500 per Trp, 1490 per Tyr). The asymmetric unit mass adds every entity in the deposited model except water.

    Details for the methods (optional)
    Final sample and quality

    A gel with lanes labelled U and I (uninduced, induced), plus S, P or time points, is shown as the expression gel. Pick Expression or Lysis for any lane in the gel's lane list.

    From a GenBank file the tool finds the ORF, its promoter, RBS and terminator, splits the tag at the protease site and checks the ORF against the Protein section. SnapGene and Benchling both export GenBank (.gb).

    With the culture volume set, the summary table gives the yield per liter, and per gram of cells when the cell mass is set.

    Gel lanes labelled −Ulp1 and +Ulp1 (or uncut and cut, before and after) go with the cleavage step, and the after lane gives the share cleaved. Runs up to the cleavage step use the tagged form for A280 and mass, runs after it the cleaved form. Clear the arrow note on the run before it if it repeats the protease.

    NanoDrop

    Protein A280, A205 and Proteins & Labels exports from the NanoDrop One, 2000 or 8000 (.csv or .tsv) work, as do files dropped on the page. Nucleic acid readings stay in ng/µL and are left out of protein totals.

    Readings named after a pool (T3-T6) or a step (HisTrap, Q, SEC) go with that step. Replicates of a stage are averaged (mean ± SD). Mark readings taken after concentration or dialysis as Final and give the final volume, and the step's protein then comes from those.

    Concentration is A280 divided by the A280 of 1 mg/mL for the form in that step (tagged or untagged, from the Protein section), else the single A280 value set under Peak integration, else the instrument's value. Lysate, load and flowthrough readings keep their A280 only. 260/280 above 0.6 is marked, and above 0.8 points to nucleic acid, although NAD(P) binding proteins can reach 0.9 from bound nucleotide alone.

    Pool highlight

    Peak integration

    Drag across the chromatogram to integrate a region, or click Detect peaks under the figure. Gaussian fit splits overlapping peaks over one shared baseline. ÄKTA pure and start UV cells are 2 mm.

    Size exclusion (SEC)

    Apparent size comes from a straight line of log(MW) against Kav = (Ve − V₀)/(Vₜ − V₀), or against Ve when V₀ and Vₜ are left blank. Sizes outside your standards are marked ~. Shape matters in SEC, so an elongated or disordered protein runs larger than its mass.

    Gel image

    1. Crop and contrast

    Auto crop trims to the stained lanes, from just below the wells to just below the dye front. Very faint lanes can be missed, so typing one label per lane sets the lane count and refits the crop. Moving a trim slider takes over by hand.

    2. Lanes

    Lanes labeled with a fraction name (T3, B5 ...) are matched to the run that collected it, and Lysate, FT or Pool lanes join their neighbours. Pick a run in the list to override a lane. If tube names repeat after a full rack, write T5#2 for the second T5 (the #2 is not printed on the figure). The same works in pooled fractions, and a range like T28-T3 runs across the wrap.

    3. Ladder

    Found automatically and updated when the crop or lanes change. To mark by hand, click each band from the top down. Auto-detect ladder switches back.

    4. Bands and purity

    Reset all goes back to exactly what detection found: it clears bands you added or removed and all target, tagged and contaminant marks on this gel. Undo steps back one change at a time.

    5. Cleaved and uncleaved protein

    Expected sizes appear here once the protein size is known.

    6. Known proteins and contaminants

    Off until you turn it on. A band is flagged only when it is a distinct, unsaturated band inside the marked ladder, its size fits one entry (within the ladder's local accuracy), it is not one of your protein's own forms (cleaved, tagged, free tag, dimer), and the lane is from a step where that protein is plausible (E. coli co-purifiers up to IMAC, proteases and free tag after cleavage). Arrows need the band in two lanes or at 10% of one. Flags read "size ≈ ArnA" because a matching size is not an identification. Confirm by western blot or mass spec when it matters. The default list is native E. coli proteins that co-purify on IMAC, plus common purification additives. Your list is saved in this browser.

    Purification summary

      Methods text

      Protein is the A₂₈₀ area of each pool divided by the A₂₈₀ of a 1 mg/mL solution (set under Peak integration) and the flow cell path. Yield is relative to the first step. Purity is the target's share of the stain in that step's Pool lane, when one is marked.

      Bands and estimated sizes

      Click a band chip to cycle it through unmarked, untagged target, tagged form and contaminant for that lane only. × (or Delete) removes the band. Dashed chips were added by hand.

      Sizes are interpolated between ladder bands on a log scale. % of lane is each band's share of all stain detected in that lane.

      To flag bands whose size matches common E. coli co-purifiers or your protease, turn it on under 6. Known proteins and contaminants.
